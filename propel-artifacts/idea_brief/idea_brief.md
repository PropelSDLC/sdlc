## Description

An operational product for SMB operations managers who juggle multiple SaaS that consolidates fragmented workflows and eliminates repeated manual re-keying of data across tools. The product targets an 80% reduction in manual re-keying within 6-12 months.

## Problems & Solutions

### Problem 1: Fragmented workflows causing manual re-keying
**Who is affected:** SMB operations managers who juggle multiple SaaS. 
**Impact:** Fragmented workflows force manual re-keying across tools, producing repeated manual transfers and multiple workflow touchpoints that increase operational effort for SMB operations managers. Observable consequences to record during discovery include: (a) the percent of tasks that require manual re-keying, and (b) the average time spent per re-keying event. The transcript does not provide baseline values for these metrics; establishing those baselines during initial discovery is required to make impact measurable. The brief targets an 80% reduction in manual re-keying within 6-12 months measured as an 80% reduction in the percent of tasks requiring manual re-keying relative to discovery-established baselines. 
**How this product solves it:** The product reduces manual transfers and consolidates workflow touchpoints so SMB operations managers perform fewer manual re-keying steps. Success is measured as an 80% reduction in the percent of tasks requiring manual re-keying within 6-12 months versus the baselines established during initial discovery.

### Problem 2: Unclear problem definition and persona risk
**Who is affected:** Product teams and stakeholders responsible for opportunity definition and prioritization; primary customer persona identified in the interview is an SMB operations manager juggling multiple SaaS. 
**Impact:** The interview designates "define a clear customer problem and persona" as the brief's primary objective and sets a measurable target of reducing manual re-keying by 80% within 6-12 months. The transcript does not include a documented pre-deployment baseline count of manual re-keying events. As a direct, observable consequence, teams cannot: (a) produce acceptance criteria that map feature proposals to the 80% reduction target, and (b) measure progress or validate prioritization decisions against that target. This evidence gap creates a measurable risk of misaligned feature work and wasted development effort because success cannot be quantified or demonstrated against the transcript-stated metric. 
**How this product solves it:** The brief defines and locks a single target persona (SMB operations manager juggling multiple SaaS) and a concise customer problem statement (fragmented workflows forcing manual re-keying). The brief also mandates documenting a pre-deployment baseline for manual re-keying and tying prioritization and acceptance criteria to the transcript-stated success metric (80% reduction in 6-12 months); outstanding evidence gaps and assumptions are recorded in Assumptions and outstanding questions.

## Key Features

- Centralized orchestration of cross-SaaS workflows that coordinates related tasks for the SMB operations manager; Acceptance: pilot measurements per Measurable outcomes show a ≥80% reduction in manual re-key operations for the prioritized workflows within 6-12 months versus a documented pre-deployment baseline, and a documented scope limit of 1-3 prioritized workflows and 2 prioritized SaaS targets plus committed pilot customers and a documented resourcing and ownership plan (naming the metric owner and cross-functional roles) exist before pilot start.
- Automated data synchronization with configurable field mapping that maintains record consistency across connected applications; Acceptance: synchronization produces mapping conflict logs, pilot reports per Measurable outcomes demonstrate a measured decline in manual re-key events for connected records versus baseline, and a documented plan that identifies ownership for resolving mapping conflicts and any API/authentication constraints exists prior to scaling.
- Prebuilt, lightweight connectors for a prioritized set of 2-5 commonly used SMB SaaS that reduce work required to link systems; Acceptance: product documentation lists prioritized integrations, pilot customers commit to validate integrations, and the MVP delivers at least 2 connectors validated in pilot deployments with pilot reports referenced in Measurable outcomes.
- Monitoring, alerting, and exception visibility that surfaces sync failures and mapping conflicts to the operations manager; Acceptance: operational logs and notification records exist, pilot data referenced in Measurable outcomes show fewer manual re-entry incidents attributable to timely alerts versus baseline, and the pilot defines an alert-to-resolution metric and an incident owner for measured comparison against baseline.
- Reporting and analytics that quantify manual re-keying events and present progress toward the 80% reduction target over a 6-12 month window; Acceptance: a documented pre-deployment baseline, a named metric owner, a pilot rollout plan, and a delivery plan that specifies required cross-functional resources and timelines exist prior to scaling and are used to generate periodic Measurable outcomes progress reports showing counts and percentage reduction versus baseline.

## Success Criteria

- Achieve an 80% reduction in manual re-keying events within 6-12 months, measured as the count of manual re-key operations per processed transaction versus a documented pre-deployment baseline; acceptance: the baseline is recorded before deployment, a named metric owner from product or customer success publishes weekly re-key counts during the 6-12 month measurement window, and measured reduction ≥80% for the automated workflows at month 6-12 (the transcript specifies the 80% target but does not name a metric owner; see Assumptions and outstanding questions). 
- Reduce weekly person-hours spent on manual data entry across SMB operations teams within 6-12 months, measured as total staffed hours for manual entry on automated workflows versus the documented baseline; acceptance: teams report weekly staffed-hours and staffed-hours for those automated workflows decline by ≥80% relative to baseline, demonstrating proportional time savings aligned to the 80% re-keying reduction target (the transcript does not specify an absolute hours target; see Assumptions and outstanding questions).
- Decrease re-keying-related data errors and post-entry correction incidents within 6-12 months, measured as absolute error counts and error rate per 1,000 processed items for the automated workflows versus baseline; acceptance: error count and error rate for those workflows decline in proportion to the achieved reduction in manual re-keying events and the measurement period shows a statistically significant decrease versus baseline (the transcript does not provide a numeric error-reduction target; see Assumptions and outstanding questions).
- Reach measurable operational adoption within 6-12 months, tracked as the percentage of eligible transactions routed through the new workflow or the percentage of SMB operations teams using the capability versus baseline; acceptance: 1-3 pilot customers and prioritized integrations execute pilot runs, pilots publish weekly adoption and outcome reports, and pilot workflows demonstrate ≥80% of eligible transactions routed through the new workflow for pilots before wider rollout; prerequisite: a documented baseline, a named metric owner, committed pilot customers, prioritized integrations, and an explicit resourcing/ownership plan to mitigate risks (API limits/auth, schema drift, authentication/permissions, edge-case mappings, and missing resource commitments)-outstanding owners and questions are recorded in Assumptions and outstanding questions.

## Scope

**In:** 
- Capabilities that directly eliminate manual re-keying events for the SMB operations manager persona identified in the interview (SMB operations manager juggling multiple SaaS). Acceptance criterion: measurable contribution to the 80% reduction in manual re-keying events defined in Measurable outcomes, measured against a documented pre-deployment baseline.
- Automated data-transfer features that replicate operational data between commonly used SMB SaaS to remove discrete duplicate-entry steps. Acceptance criterion: each feature maps a documented re-keying use case to an automated data transfer and reduces the count of manual re-key operations for that use case versus the Measurable outcomes baseline.
- Workflow-level automations and triggers that replace manual transcription for operational tasks performed by SMB operations managers (for example: data transfers, status updates, handoffs). Acceptance criterion: each automation executes the previously manual task without human transcription and reduces manual re-keying events for the associated workflow per the Measurable outcomes baseline.
- Measurement and reporting capabilities that track progress against the 80% re-keying reduction target within the 6-12 month window. Acceptance criterion: the team records a pre-deployment baseline and reports a recurring metric (count of manual re-key operations per processed transaction) with time-bound deltas to stakeholders as defined in Measurable outcomes.
- Pre-built connectors and integration patterns for commonly used SMB SaaS where the connector reduces operational re-keying effort. Acceptance criterion: each connector links a named SaaS to at least one operational workflow and demonstrates a reduction in manual re-key events for that workflow against the Measurable outcomes baseline.

**Out:** 
- Deep replacement of core SaaS platforms (full platform substitution or re-platforming). Reason: interview priorities focus on eliminating manual re-keying across existing SaaS used by SMB operations managers rather than replacing those platforms.
- Enterprise-only custom integrations and one-off bespoke engineering work for large customers. Reason: interview priorities target SMB operational efficiency and the 6-12 month, 80% re-keying reduction objective; enterprise-only work does not align with the target persona or measurable timeframe.
- Non-operational use cases (for example, product marketing analytics or executive financial consolidation) that do not directly eliminate manual operational re-keying. Reason: the core problem and selected persona center on operational workflows and manual entry between operational SaaS.
- Detailed technical design, platform re-architecture, or implementation plans. Reason: the idea brief scope targets business capabilities to reduce re-keying and leaves downstream design and engineering work to subsequent specification phases (see Capabilities to reduce manual re-keying and Measurable outcomes for capability and outcome alignment).
